Where we deliver

We deliver custom rigid boxes to the United Kingdom, the United States (all 50 states), Canada and most European countries. If your country is not listed, ask us and we will check.

Delivery costs

Bulk quotes include delivery to the address you give us. Sample shipping is charged separately and confirmed before you pay. Deliveries to remote areas may cost more; if so, we tell you in your quote.

Delivery times

Delivery time depends on your location, the order size and the shipping method. We give you an estimated production and delivery time with your quote and confirm it when you approve the proof. Dates are estimates, and we will tell you straight away if anything changes.

How we pack your order

Rigid boxes ship fully built. We pack them in strong export cartons with corner protection so the edges, wraps and finishes arrive undamaged. Cartons are labelled with the contents and quantity.

Customs, duties and taxes

For deliveries outside the UK, we prepare the commercial invoice and export documents. Import duties, VAT or sales taxes in your country, if any, are the responsibility of the receiver unless your quote says otherwise. We explain what to expect before you order.

Tracking

You receive tracking details as soon as your order is dispatched.

Damaged or missing deliveries

Please check your delivery when it arrives. If cartons are damaged, note it with the courier and take photos. Report any damaged or missing boxes to us within 7 days of delivery. See our Returns policy for how we put it right.
