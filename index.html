      Want to hold one before ordering?

      Fair enough, most people do. We can make a single sample of any style so you can check the board, the fit and the finish yourself.

      • The sample price depends on size, style and finishes, and is confirmed before you pay.
      • A single sample costs more per piece than bulk.
