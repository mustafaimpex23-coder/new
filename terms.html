Before production we send a dieline and digital proof. Please check sizes, spelling, colours and logo positions carefully. Production starts only after you approve the proof in writing, and we make the boxes as approved. Colours on screen can differ slightly from printed and wrapped materials.

Orders and payment

The minimum order is 50 pieces per design. An order is confirmed when you approve the proof and pay the agreed deposit or amount. Payment terms are set out in your quote. Samples are paid in advance, and sample shipping is charged separately.
