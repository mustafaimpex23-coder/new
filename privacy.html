Who we are

This website is operated by WNP Enterprise Ltd, trading as Luxora Packaging, registered in England & Wales (Company No. 16012866), Unit 5, Industrial Estate, Wednesfield Way, Well Lane, Wolverhampton WV11 1XP, United Kingdom. In the United States we operate as The Providers LLC, 2335 Broadway, Astoria, NY 11106. In this policy, "we" and "us" means Luxora Packaging. We are the controller of the personal data described here.

What we collect

  • Details you send us through our forms, email or WhatsApp: your name, email address, phone number, company, country and the details of your packaging enquiry.
  • Order information, such as delivery addresses and invoices.
  • If you accept analytics cookies, anonymous information about how you use the website.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare quotes, proofs and samples.
  • To make and deliver your order and handle any issues.
  • To keep business and accounting records as required by law.
  • To understand which pages are useful, if you accept analytics cookies.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not send marketing emails unless you ask us to.

Legal basis

We process your data to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, to perform a contract, to meet legal obligations, and, for analytics cookies only, with your consent.

Cookies and analytics

We only use Google Analytics cookies if you click "Accept" on the cookie bar. Your choice is saved in your browser. If you click "No thanks", no analytics cookies are set. You can change your choice by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Who we share it with

We share data only where needed to run our business: couriers that deliver your order, our production partners (who receive the box details, not your personal contact details where avoidable), payment and accounting providers, and messaging services such as WhatsApp or email when you choose to contact us that way. Some of these providers may process data outside the UK, with appropriate safeguards in place.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to an order are kept for up to 2 years. Order and invoice records are kept for 6 years to meet UK accounting rules.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive a copy of it. Email LuxorapackagingGlobal@gmail.com and we will reply within one month.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
