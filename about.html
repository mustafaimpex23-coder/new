About Luxora Packaging

Luxury rigid boxes for brands that are too small for the big factories, but still want a box that feels special.

Our story

Why we started

Most packaging factories are built for brands ordering tens of thousands of boxes. If you need 50 perfume boxes for a launch, or 200 gift boxes for Christmas, you are usually told the minimum is too high, or you get a box that feels like an afterthought.

We started Luxora Packaging to fix that. We make proper rigid boxes (thick greyboard, wrapped papers, hidden magnets, fitted inserts) for small and growing brands, from just 50 pieces.

We work with fragrance houses, jewellers, candle makers, chocolatiers, skincare brands and corporate gift teams across the UK, USA, Canada and Europe. Every order goes through the same people, from the first message to the day the boxes arrive.

A note from Luxora Packaging

An order of 50 or 100 boxes is completely fine by us. You will talk to the same team from your first message to the day your boxes arrive. If a style will not suit your product, or a cheaper finish would look just as good, we will tell you before you spend the money.
