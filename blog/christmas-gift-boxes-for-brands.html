Christmas Gift Boxes for Brands: Plan Your Holiday Packaging

  • By The Luxora Packaging Team
  • 3 min read

Every year the same thing happens. In September, Christmas feels far away. By late October, brands are asking whether they can still get gift boxes made in time. The good news is that rigid gift boxes are very achievable for the holidays, even in small quantities. The trick is to make a handful of decisions early, so nothing is rushed at the end.

Start earlier than feels necessary

A custom rigid box goes through a quote, a dieline and proof, an optional sample, production, checking and delivery. None of these steps is slow on its own, but they add up, and everyone else is ordering at the same time. We suggest starting the conversation at least eight to ten weeks before you need the boxes in hand, and earlier if you want a sample first.

Tip

Work backwards from the date your gifts need to leave your warehouse, not from Christmas Day. Then add a week of breathing space.

Choose a style that feels like a gift

Some box styles simply feel more festive than others. For Christmas gifting, three stand out:

  • Magnetic flip-top boxes are the safe favourite. The lid snaps shut on hidden magnets and the whole box feels solid and generous.
  • Sliding drawer boxes add a little theatre. A ribbon pull on the tray makes opening feel like unwrapping.
  • Lid and base boxes are ideal for hampers and clothing, and a shoulder-neck version gives a stepped, premium edge.

If you are unsure, tell us what goes inside and who receives it. A corporate client gift and a retail gift set often suit different styles.

Festive finishes that are worth it

You do not need every finish to make a box feel seasonal. Usually one strong choice does more than three weak ones.

  • Gold or rose-gold foil on a deep green, burgundy, navy or black wrap is the classic Christmas look.
  • A satin ribbon, either as a pull tab or a tied bow, makes a plain box instantly giftable.
  • A printed message inside the lid costs less than most people expect and is what people remember.

A useful rule: keep the outside of the box timeless and put the seasonal message on a sleeve, sticker or inside print. Then you can reuse the same box after December.

Inserts keep gift sets looking perfect

A gift set that arrives with everything slid into one corner loses its magic. Fitted inserts solve this. EVA foam cut-outs hold bottles, candles and jars tight. Card dividers work well for mixed hampers and are fully recyclable. For loose gifts, a bed of crinkle paper fills the gaps and looks generous.

How many boxes to order

Our minimum is 50 pieces per design, which suits most small brands testing a Christmas range. The price per box drops as quantity rises, because set-up costs are shared across more boxes. If you are torn between two quantities, ask us to price both. The gap is often smaller than you think, and spare boxes can carry you into Valentine's Day or next Christmas.

Tip

If you plan several gift sets, try to keep the box size the same and change only the insert. One box design, several products, one minimum order.

A simple Christmas checklist

  1. Decide what goes inside and measure it.
  2. Pick a style and one or two finishes.
  3. Ask for a quote with your delivery date.
  4. Approve the dieline and proof quickly.
  5. Order a sample only if you have time for it.
  6. Plan where the boxes will be stored when they arrive.

That is genuinely all it takes. Start early, keep the design focused, and your gift boxes will be one less thing to worry about in December.
