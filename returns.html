Custom-made items

Every box we make is produced to your size, design and artwork. Because of this, custom-made items cannot be returned or refunded for a change of mind, and the usual consumer cancellation rights do not apply to personalised goods.

Faulty or damaged boxes

If boxes arrive faulty, damaged in transit, or clearly different from the proof you approved, we will put it right. A fault means a real defect, such as boxes made to the wrong size, the wrong colour or finish, broken magnets, or crushed boxes. Normal handmade variation described in our Terms is not a fault.

How to report a problem

  1. Email LuxorapackagingGlobal@gmail.com within 7 days of delivery.
  2. Include your order details, the number of affected boxes and clear photos of the problem and the outer cartons.
  3. Keep the boxes and packaging until we have reviewed your claim.

How we put it right

Once we confirm the problem, we will remake and replace the affected boxes, or refund the cost of those boxes, whichever is more suitable. We cover the cost of any return shipping we ask for.

Samples

Samples are made one-off to your request and are non-refundable, unless the sample is faulty or damaged in transit.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel free of charge before you approve the proof. After approval, materials are cut and printed for you, so cancellation may not be possible, or costs already incurred will be charged.

Contact

Email LuxorapackagingGlobal@gmail.com, or write to WNP Enterprise Ltd, Unit 5, Industrial Estate, Wednesfield Way, Well Lane, Wolverhampton WV11 1XP. US customers can also email info.theprovidersllc@gmail.com.
